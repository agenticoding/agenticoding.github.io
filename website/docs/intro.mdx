## Learn to run the machine.

An agent is a machine. It keeps working after you stop touching it, and it builds exactly what you asked for — including the parts you didn't mean. Every powerful tool has offered the same bargain: useless in careless hands, transformative in skilled ones.

> For the first time, the limit on what you can build is your intent, not your hands.

That skill is the whole prize. Operate the machine well and the labor moves to it while the judgment stays with you.
